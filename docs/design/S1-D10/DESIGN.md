## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Intent
This design system crafts an affectionate, tranquil haven engineered for two people staying connected across distance. Rooted in tender intimacy, domestic stillness, and evening calm, the experience consciously rejects urgency paradigms—foregoing streaks, aggressive notifications, and high-frequency gamification. Instead, the interface evokes the tactile comfort of wrapping oneself in a cashmere blanket beneath gentle twilight: warm rose blush, delicate petal tones, velvety night plums, and quiet candlelit embers.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
The visual philosophy pairs **Tactile Warmth** with **Nocturnal Soft-Glassmorphism**:
- **Tactile Cushioning:** Interactive surfaces emulate soft stationery paper, memory polaroids, velvet cushions, and smooth ceramics with rounded geometries.
- **Ambient Night Chamber:** Velvet plum and deep indigo tones establish a cozy room environment coordinated with warm illustrated domestic scenes.
- **Constrained Luminous Embers:** Warm amber and soft honey glows are reserved exclusively for low-intensity ambient accents—window starlight, bedside lanterns, and quiet "thinking of you" signals.
- **Thumb-Centered Interaction:** Controls, floating panels, and quick affection gestures reside primarily within the lower thumb zone to facilitate unhurried, single-handed interaction during late-night relaxation.

## Colors

### Chromatic Roles
- **Primary (`#f472b6` - Soft Petal Rose):** The core emotional anchor. Governs primary action buttons, key affection triggers, active outlines, and focused interface boundaries.
- **Secondary (`#fb7185` - Warm Coral Rose):** Expressive warmth used for reciprocal partner pings, interactive hearts, active navigational highlights, and tender gesture feedback.
- **Tertiary (`#fbbf24` - Bedside Glow Amber):** Strictly functional secondary radiance. Reserved for lantern toggles, candle wicks, starlight through the window, and gentle "thinking of you" embers. Amber must never dominate large container fills or standard text blocks.
- **Neutral (`#190f24` - Velvet Night Canvas):** The foundational dark plum background. Layered with deeper recesses (`#13091e`) and warmer elevated night tones (`#21172c`, `#2f2242`) to create cozy environmental depth without cold, sterile grays.

### Named Pastel & Ambient Tones
- **`blush-soft` (`#fbcfe8`):** Readable text contrast against deep plum containers, message bubble fills, and pill badges.
- **`blush-petal` (`#fda4af`):** Warm secondary labels, interactive borders, and memory note headers.
- **`blush-mist` (`#fff1f2`):** Primary display text, photo borders, and crisp high-contrast micro-details.
- **`amber-soft` (`#fef3c7`):** Diffusion glow tint and subtle candlelit highlights.

### Accessibility & Luminance Hierarchy
- Text contrast conforms strictly to WCAG AA on nocturnal backgrounds, pairing `#fff1f2` and `#fbcfe8` over `#190f24` and `#21172c`.
- Avoid harsh red error banners; validation notices leverage muted rose-plum tones with gentle descriptive typography.
- Chromatic distribution target: 65% velvet night plum surfaces, 25% blush/petal rose structures, 10% warm text accents, with amber radiance limited to ≤5% of visual footprint.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Headlines & Expressive Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern geometric proportions softened by organic humanist curves. It introduces welcoming structure to greeting titles, sheet headers, and modal labels without looking corporate.
- **Narrative Copy & Memories (Nunito Sans):** Rounded terminal endings create comfortable, warm readability for intimate letters, shared logs, daily thoughts, and microcopy.

### Typesetting Rules
- Maintain generous line heights on continuous narrative text to promote effortless reading under low-light evening environments.
- High-level headlines on dark surfaces default to `blush-mist` (`#fff1f2`), while secondary body paragraphs use `blush-soft` (`#fbcfe8`) to ensure soft contrast that prevents eye fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Structure & Grid
- **Fluid Layout with Safe Anchors:** Built around an 8px spatial module. Mobile screens leverage a 4-column responsive grid with `1rem` (16px) margins and `0.75rem` (12px) gutters. Tablet and larger form factors adapt to an 8-column layout capped at a maximum sanctuary canvas width of 680px for focused intimacy.
- **Thumb Reach Architecture:** The primary interaction plane rests within the lower 50% of the mobile viewport. Dynamic scene elements (sofa, rug, partner avatars) anchor along the bottom, while celestial windows, cork boards, and ambient indicators occupy the upper canvas.
- **Tap Tolerances:** All interactive controls maintain a minimum target dimension of 44×44px, separated by a minimum gap of `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Luminance & Layering
Rather than standard drop shadows, depth is communicated through velvety tonal layering, frosted glass translucency, and gentle ambient radiance:

- **Base Chamber (Level 0):** Velvet nocturnal base (`#190f24` merging into `#13091e`).
- **Resting Sheets & Cork Panels (Level 1):** Semi-translucent plum glass (`rgba(33, 23, 44, 0.72)`) with a `16px` backdrop blur and a fine border outline in `rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.16)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Modals (Level 2):** Elevated night containers (`#2f2242` at 88% opacity) accompanied by an ambient plum shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(10, 6, 18, 0.65)`.
- **Ambient Golden Embers (Lantern & Starlight):** A localized soft bloom (`0 0 24px 6px rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.22)`) reserved strictly for lantern active states, window stars, and warm notification bells.
- **Affection Pulse Halo:** Interactive affection pills emit a soft rose glow (`0 0 20px 4px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.28)`) that animates with a slow, calming pulse.

## Shapes

### Contour Hierarchy
The shape language uses roundedness level `2` (0.5rem baseline) to maintain soft, welcoming silhouettes that echo pillows, domestic upholstery, and keepsake polaroids:

- **Standard Components:** Input fields, memory cards, and nested sheets use `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) and `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px).
- **Floating Controls & Action Buttons:** Chips, status tags, and action triggers feature full pill contours (`9999px`) to offer approachable, tap-friendly touchpoints.
- **Strictly No Sharp Corners:** No corner is left unrounded; hard 90° edges contradict the protective, comforting intent of the interface.

## Components

### Buttons & Affection Triggers
- **Primary Affection Button:** Solid fill in soft petal rose (`#f472b6`) with midnight plum typography (`#190f24`). Finished with `rounded-full`, accompanied by a subtle blush outer bloom. On press, applies a gentle elastic scale (0.97).
- **Secondary Pillow Action:** Frosted nocturnal plum fill (`rgba(47, 34, 66, 0.5)`) framed by a subtle petal pink border (`rgba(251, 207, 232, 0.28)`), displaying text in `#fda4af`.
- **Ambient Lamp Toggle:** Pill switch displaying a soft amber glow (`#fbbf24`) with `amber-soft` (`#fef3c7`) highlights when active, indicating domestic presence and room illumination.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`) using translucent indigo-plum surfaces with `#fbcfe8` text and a gentle 1px border (`rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.2)`).
- **"Thinking of You" Indicator:** Features an amber ember dot (`#fbbf24`) paired with `label-sm` typography in `#fef3c7`.

### Memory Cards & Sticky Notes
- **Photo Polaroids:** Thick, warm card frames (`#fff1f2`) surrounding shared memories, subtly rotated ($\pm 1.5^\circ$) with rounded corners (16px) and soft ambient shadows.
- **Sticky Love Notes:** Delicate surfaces in `blush-soft` (`#fbcfe8`) or `blush-mist` (`#fff1f2`) carrying handwritten-style notes, pinned to the corkboard with small rose-tinted pins.

### Form Inputs & Chat Fields
- **Thought Input:** Pill-shaped text box filled with deep plum glass (`rgba(25, 15, 36, 0.7)`), bounded by a hairline border (`rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.2)`), with placeholder copy rendered in `#dac0c9`.
- **Focused State:** Border brightens to `#f472b6` with an accompanying subtle aura (`0 0 12px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.25)`).

### Toggles, Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- **Selection Controls:** Circular and pill-shaped selectors filled with `#21172c` when unselected and filled with `#f472b6` with midnight plum checkmarks when selected.
