## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Intent
This design system crafts an affectionate, tranquil haven engineered for two people staying connected across distance. Rooted in tender intimacy, gentle domestic stillness, and evening comfort, the experience actively dismantles the urgency paradigms of modern software. There are no streaks, punitive badges, or engagement quotas. Instead, the UI evokes wrapping oneself in a cashmere throw beneath dim twilight, enveloped in soft blush, rose petal, and powdery tones, with an ambient amber nightlight resting quietly in the corner.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
The visual philosophy unites **Tactile Pastel Warmth** with **Atmospheric Evening Glassmorphism**:
- **Tactile Cushioning:** Surfaces evoke brushed fleece, cotton paper, velvet cushions, and smooth rounded ceramics. 
- **Chamber Glow & Subdued Amber:** The primary emotional canvas is dominated by soothing tones of rose petal, powder blush, and deep bedtime plum. Warm amber and lantern honey tones are repurposed strictly as subtle secondary illuminations—a gentle bedside lamp or warm window glow.
- **Single-Thumb Intimacy:** Interactions are weighted heavily toward the lower half of the screen, prioritizing relaxed, single-handed interaction during quiet, late-night unwinding.

## Colors

### Harmonic Architecture
The revised chromatic hierarchy places delicate rose, powder blush, and warm twilight plums at the forefront, reassigning amber to an accent lighting role:

- **Primary (`#f472b6` Soft Petal Rose):** The core brand anchor. Used for primary touch targets, heartfelt tokens, active outlines, and prominent emotional highlights.
- **Secondary (`#fb7185` Warm Coral Rose):** Expressive warmth used for direct partner gestures, intimate stamps, active tab states, and reactive hugs.
- **Tertiary (`#fbbf24` Bedside Glow Amber):** Strictly reserved for subtle warm light emissions, star glows through the window, nightstand lamp reflections, and candlelit accents. It does not dominate primary surfaces or primary button fills.
- **Neutral (`#1a1025` Deep Velvet Plum):** The atmospheric dark canvas representing evening calm. Supported by layered violet-tinted night shades (`#130d1e`, `#231832`, `#2f2242`) that avoid clinical desaturated grays.
- **Pastel Tints (`#fbcfe8`, `#fda4af`, `#fff1f2`):** Provide readable text contrast, pill badges, tender message bubbles, and soft layered cards.

### Chromatic Governance
- **No Alarm Red:** Error states and destructive prompts are softened to muted dusk rust tones rather than piercing alarms.
- **Luminosity Ratio:** In any evening scene, rose and blush tones constitute roughly 70% of thematic accents, deep plum backgrounds 25%, and amber radiance remains constrained to $\le 5\%$ for pure ambient bloom.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Headlines & Expressive Labels:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides rounded geometry, gentle curves, and friendly warmth, ensuring headers feel intimate rather than corporate.
- **Narrative Copy & Notes:** **Nunito Sans** incorporates rounded humanist terminals, offering effortless legibility for long-form love letters, memory captions, and cozy microcopy.

### Typographic Atmosphere
- Headings maintain generous line height to invite relaxed reading.
- Text tones avoid pure white `#ffffff`, settling on blush-mist (`#fff1f2`) for headlines and soft petal pink (`#fbcfe8`) for secondary body text against nocturnal purple surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

### Spatial Rhythms
The spatial rhythm relies on an 8px base module (`space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px) paired with generous breathing room to evoke an unhurried sanctuary.

- **Thumb-Zone Anchor:** Core controls, intimate messaging drawers, and reaction bars sit comfortably within the bottom 55% of the mobile viewport.
- **Canvas Framing:** Illustrated room elements conform to dynamic vertical viewport units, pinning the sofa, pet cushion, and carpet along the bottom edge while extending the night window and celestial sky upward on taller displays.
- **Interactive Boundaries:** Action targets maintain an absolute minimum touch zone of $48\times48\text{ px}$ with at least `space-sm` (8px) gap between adjacent tappable items.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Luminance & Velvet Layers
Visual hierarchy is defined through soft translucent layering, gentle border luminescence, and localized glows:

1. **Backdrop Chamber (Level 0):** Velvet nocturnal plum (`#1a1025` transitioning to `#130d1e`), deep and restful.
2. **Resting Sheets & Cork Panels (Level 1):** Semi-opaque plum-violet glass (`rgba(35, 24, 50, 0.70)`) with `16px` backdrop blur and a whisper-thin rose border (`rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.15)`).
3. **Floating Notes & Modals (Level 2):** Elevated layered containers (`rgba(47, 34, 66, 0.85)`) framed by a diffuse dusk shadow: `0 12px 36px -6px rgba(10, 6, 16, 0.60)`.
4. **Bedside Lamp & Window Bloom (Secondary Light):** A soft, ambient golden glow (`0 0 24px 4px rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.20)`) strictly localized to illuminate lampshades, celestial window stars, or candle flames.
5. **Presence & Affection Pulse (Primary Aura):** Active partner touchpoints glow with a gentle blush halo (`0 0 20px 4px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.30)`), breathing slowly at 0.15 Hz.

## Shapes

### Contour Language
A roundedness level of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline) establishes a tactile, soft-edged world reminiscent of pillows, worn wood shelves, and rounded photo polaroids.

- **Cards & Notes:** Use `rounded-lg` (`16px`) to emulate soft stationery paper and memory pins.
- **Pills & Bubbles:** Interactive action triggers, reaction pills, and active status tags use pill-shaped finishes (`9999px`) to invite gentle thumb taps.
- **Strictly No Sharp Corners:** No interactive element carries zero-radius corners. All edges convey tactile comfort.

## Components

### Buttons & Quick Reactions
- **Primary Affection Button:** Coated in soft petal rose (`#f472b6`) with midnight plum text (`#1a1025`) and a subtle rose bloom rim. Upon tap, it gently scales down (0.97) with a plush spring easing.
- **Secondary Pillow Action:** Framed with a delicate blush border (`rgba(251, 207, 232, 0.3)`) over a soft translucent plum glass fill (`rgba(47, 34, 66, 0.4)`), colored in `#fda4af`.
- **Glow Toggle (Lamp Control):** Subtle amber illumination (`#fbbf24`) applied exclusively to lamp icons or light switches to represent domestic warmth without overtaking the screen.

### Corkboard, Memory Cards & Sticky Notes
- **Sticky Memos:** Hues inspired by the pinned notes: soft blush pink (`#fda4af`), creamy blush mist (`#fff1f2`), and pastel peach. Framed with rounded corners (`16px`) and subtle pin-head badges.
- **Polaroid Frames:** Warm off-white borders (`#fff1f2`) housing partner photos, tilted slightly ($\pm 1.5^\circ$) to maintain an organic, tactile scrapbooking feeling.

### Text Inputs & Expression Fields
- **Chat & Note Inputs:** Rounded pill bowls filled with muted plum glass (`rgba(26, 16, 37, 0.60)`), bordered with low-opacity rose (`rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.25)`).
- **Focus State:** Transitions into a glowing soft petal outline (`#f472b6`) backed by a diffuse aura (`0 0 12px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.20)`).

### Presence Indicators & Gentle Notifications
- **Affection Embers:** Floating rose heart particles (`#f472b6`, `#fb7185`) that drift softly around the avatar couple or corkboard when thoughts are shared.
- **Window Glow:** The celestial arch window shifts its inner amber starlight tint (`#fbbf24`) to signify whether both partners are currently resting in the room together.
